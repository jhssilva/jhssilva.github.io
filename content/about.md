I'm a backend software engineer at [Datadog](https://www.datadoghq.com) in Paris, working on the error deobfuscation platform: the systems that turn minified JavaScript and native crash reports into readable stack traces.

I have 7+ years of experience building reliable, cost-efficient distributed systems. At Datadog, I shipped [JavaScript Debug IDs](https://docs.datadoghq.com/real_user_monitoring/guide/upload-javascript-source-maps/), cutting duplicated symbol ingestion by 95%+, delivered native crash symbolication across Windows, macOS, iOS and Linux, and cut on-call pages by 98%.
